Complete Workflow for Printing ERP Process
Phase 1 — Customer Initiation & Pre-Sales Very as per job types
This phase captures customer requirements and prepares costing.

ENQ_JOB  →  Enquiry Creation
      ↓
ENQ_EST  →  Estimation / Costing
      ↓
QUOT     →  Quotation Generation
      ↓
QUOT_APPR → Quotation Approval
Optional financial initiation:

ADV_PAY → Advance Payment (If required before job start)
Phase 2 — Job Creation & Approval
Once quotation is approved, the job is formally created.

 #Job Card (job order) 

JOB_CREATE → Job Creation
        ↓
JOB_APPROVAL → Job Approval
Phase 3 — Design & Pre-Press
Artwork preparation and customer validation.

DES_DTP → Designing / DTP
     ↓
PROOF → Client Proof Approval
     ↓
PRE_PRESS → Plate Making
Phase 4 — Procurement & Material Handling
Material procurement and quality checks.

PROC → Material Procurement
   ↓
GRN → Goods Receipt Note
   ↓
QC_IN → Incoming QC
   ↓
STORE_ISSUE → Material Issue
Optional reverse flow:

STORE_RETURN → Material Return
Phase 5 — Production Planning
Manufacturing preparation activities.

JOB_PLAN → Production Planning
     ↓
JOB_SCHED → Job Scheduling
     ↓
JOB_CARD → Job Card Generation
Phase 6 — Production Execution
Core manufacturing steps.

CUT → Paper Cutting
   ↓
PRINT → Printing
   ↓
QC_PROC → In-Process QC
   ↓
DRY → Drying
Phase 7 — Post-Press Finishing
Final shaping and finishing.

POST_PRESS → Post Press Finishing
      ↓
FOLD → Folding
      ↓
BIND → Binding
      ↓
TRIM → Final Trim
      ↓
QC_POST → Post Press QC
Phase 8 — Packing & Dispatch
Shipment preparation and delivery.

PACK → Packing
   ↓
LOAD → Loading
   ↓
CHALLAN → Delivery Challan
   ↓
GATE_PASS → Gate Pass
   ↓
DISPATCH → Dispatch
   ↓
DELIVERY_CONF → Delivery Confirmation
Phase 9 — Billing & Finance
Customer billing and payment tracking.

BILL → Billing
   ↓
PAY_REC → Payment Receipt
Optional financial adjustments:

CREDIT_NOTE → Credit Note
DEBIT_NOTE  → Debit Note
Phase 10 — Costing & Closure
Final financial analysis and job closure.

WASTE_ENTRY → Wastage Entry
        ↓
COST_FINAL → Final Costing
        ↓
PROFIT_ANALYSIS → Profit Analysis
        ↓
JOB_CLOSE → Job Closure
        ↓
JOB_ARCHIVE → Job Archive

Parallel / Optional Flow Conditions
Important for ERP workflow engine logic.

Conditional Paths
If advance required:
QUOT_APPR → ADV_PAY → JOB_CREATE
If material rejected:
QC_IN → PROC (Re-procurement)
If proof rejected:
PROOF → DES_DTP
If QC failed:
QC_PROC → PRINT
QC_POST → POST_PRESS
If delivery short:
DELIVERY_CONF → CREDIT_NOTE
